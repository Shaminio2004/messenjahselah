    About

    The Messenger

    “I see reggae music as a conduit that brings us to a deeper innerstanding of our divine purpose. It is becoming a movement, where reggae is not just dance music but a vehicle for change and growth.”

    Messenjah Selah grew up in the hills outside Kingston, Jamaica, absorbing reggae from the radio, record shops, churches and sound systems. At 15 he moved to the U.S. Virgin Islands, where he formed an a cappella group that grew into a gospel reggae band, with Selah on lead vocals and guitar. That is when he knew music was his life’s path.

    In Atlanta he began recording as a solo artist in 1997, earning Best New Male Vocalist (1999) and Best New Vocalist (2001) at the Atlanta Caribbean Awards. He has shared the stage with Beres Hammond, Luciano, Sizzla, Buju Banton and Shaggy.

    Returning to Jamaica to record, he released “I Will Survive” featuring Luciano and “Joy” featuring Sizzla, both on his debut album Whosoever Will (2003). He followed with Breaking Babylon Curse (2009), the mixtape Roots and Branches (2012) and the EP Coming Home to Me (2014). His songs speak to relationships, culture, the environment, health, mindset, and social and political life.

    Today, as DivinityOne, Selah carries the message beyond the stage as a musical healer, meditation guide and heart-space mindset coach. “Life coaching on and off the stage comes naturally to me,” he says. “Transformation is my passion.”
